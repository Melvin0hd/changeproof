## Transitions and export

Use simple cuts between baseline/source/validation and let the source-to-claim identity continue through the genuine UI motion. Never animate a passed applicability gate in the recorded ESCALATE case. No celebration or fabricated progress percentage. Replay may be recorded again because it uses cached evidence; do not start a fresh provider review.

Target 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264/yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz, English narration and captions, fast-start MP4, total strictly below three minutes. Preserve the original caption timing during the reused film; the epilogue has no invented narration. No third-party music. Existing narration is locally synthesized Microsoft Zira, not a cloned person.

## Human review before upload

Watch both motion studies at normal speed and listen to the full candidate. Check captions, source URL legibility, model name, factual labels and publication rights. Numerical audio/decode checks are not proof that every word has been heard. Choose the final visual direction and whether to record the full D1 scene plan. Upload and final Devpost submission require separate approval.
